Four bounded real `gpt-5.6-sol` executions used public material only. No Notion,
service database mutation, private event data or mail were involved. The exact
inputs, outputs and token events are retained outside the repository and sync
folders. This is a small same-day diagnostic, not a quality-accuracy percentage
or a causal A/B comparison. The reader's complaint remains the calibration target.

The discovery source identities were spot-checked on the original journal and
research-team pages; this was not a full independent audit of every candidate.
The summaries used the old release's frozen public evidence, not invented samples.
They tested explanation only and did not endorse those papers as good selections.

One subsequent writing-guidance revision clarifies the explainer role and gives
conditional plain-language examples rather than merely asking to define jargon.
It adds no validator, review round or whole-edition block. It was **not** subjected
to additional local model executions; the next deployed end-to-end issue is the
next observation. Do not claim its quality was established by these four calls.
